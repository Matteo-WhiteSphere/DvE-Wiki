Add a staff account

Creates the login Role.Name (e.g. Editor.Rapha) with a temporary password that you send privately; it must be changed at the first login.

Create several staff accounts

One username per line, written as Role.Name (Owner / Admin / Editor). Accounts that already exist are skipped. Each account gets a temporary password, shown only once below, that must be changed at the first login.

Users

owner creates owners and admins · admin manages editors, deletes pages · editor creates and edits pages · disabled cannot sign in

Changing the role also renames the login (e.g. Editor.Pyro → Admin.Pyro). Reset password gives a new temporary password and signs the person out everywhere. Owners cannot be changed from here.

Activity log

Every page change and account change is recorded here. Entries cannot be edited or deleted.
