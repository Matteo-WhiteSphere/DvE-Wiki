The road from Italy

Hardline communist is mutually exclusive with Leninist and Trotskyist Alliance. Stalinist Coup gives SRI all of Italy's technology, units, stockpiles and states, keeps Rome as capital, adds 20% authoritarian socialism and 20 "Guardia di Stato" divisions, and loads SRI_focus.

3. Lore & Geopolitical Goals

Background: Rifondazione, Potere al Popolo and the old cadres refuse both the "Trotskyists" of the north and the "Black Barons" of Latina. From Naples they rebuild a party with a state, and look to Beijing for weapons and investors.

Short-term goals: unite the communists in the PCI-U, defeat the Internationale, the Republic and the RSI, take Rome.

Long-term goals: a Gosplan economy or Chinese-style market socialism, the Soviet Army, and an alliance with China.

7. Unique Events & Mechanics

  • Chinese support: Request Chinese Forces makes China far more willing to send volunteers; the military branch ends in an Italian-Chinese Alliance or a Sino-Italian Agreement.
  • Victory: after Victory Over the Social-Fascists the SRI gains cores on every Italian state.
  • After a coup: an SRI born from the Stalinist Coup already holds Rome and is at peace, so Victory Over the Social-Fascists is available as soon as its prerequisites are done.

The six-faction civil war (ITA, INT, SRI, RSI, LOM, TYR) is in the mod but, in the current version, it can only be started from the debug decisions: no focus triggers it yet.
