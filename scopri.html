The Future is in Your Hands

In Dragon vs Eagle, you are not just an observer. You lead a nation through a world on the brink. You will have to manage unstable alliances, quell internal revolts, and navigate a deep and complex political system. Every choice, from a party's popularity to the approval of a single reform, will have ripple effects on the global landscape.

Civil Wars & Global Crises

Italian Civil War

Italy can plunge into chaos. Factions include:

  • RSI: Fascists, allied with Germany.
  • Kingdom of the South: Monarchists, allied with the Allies.
  • Partisans / CLN: Communists, backed by the USSR.

Russian Civil War

Post-Stalin succession leads to the fragmentation of Russia:

  • Stalinists / Hardliners: Control Moscow.
  • Reformers / Khrushchevites: Seek opening.
  • White Nationalists: Dream of the Tsar's return.
  • Regional Separatisms: Caucasus, Ukraine, etc.
