3. Lore & Geopolitical Goals

Background: Italy enters 2025 under Giorgia Meloni's centre-right government while the radical left organises in Genoa's squares and factories. In autumn 2025 Lotta Comunista fills the city for the March Against All Wars, and in December the workers of the former Ilva plant strike against production cuts.

The collapse: when the Republic breaks apart, the Internationale holds the industrial north, the Stalinists rule from Naples, the Black Barons take Latina, and Padania and South Tyrol want nothing more to do with any of them. Rome keeps the centre, the islands and most of the south, and the generals take over.

Goal: hold the Republic together, either through the ballot box in 2027 or under the House of Savoy, and win back every state lost in the civil war.

4. National Spirits & Modifiers

Italy starts with no national spirits, only its laws: limited_exports, volunteer_only and civilian_economy.

Mattei's Mediterranean Plan is meant to add the spirit ITA_mediterranean_investments, but that spirit is not defined in the mod yet.

The civil war

When the civil war starts Italy leaves its faction (NATO), loses 30% stability, gains 10% war support and declares war on all five breakaway factions. The communists also fight the fascists and each other; Padania and South Tyrol only fight Rome.
