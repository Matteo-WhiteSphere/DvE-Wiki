3. Lore & Geopolitical Goals

Historical Background / Lore: Stubb was aligned with Kokoomus until a period of political separation, before returning to the party and accepting the request to run for President. He won the election against Petteri Orpo in the second round, securing 51.6% of the vote.

Short-term Goal: Improve the economy.

Long-term Goal: Improve relations and sign agreements with EU and Nordic nations.

Key Tensions / Primary Rivals: Russia, Belarus, and China.

7. Unique Events & Mechanics

Stagnating Economy: With the industrial path and certain political focuses, the Stagnating Economy spirit can be gradually improved through strategic reforms and economic restructuring.

Stop public debt: A series of decisions to reduce public debt, each with a small debuff. Completing enough of them grants major rewards as compensation.

Special Mechanic: Economic Stabilization & Debt Reduction
