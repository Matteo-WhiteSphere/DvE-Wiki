1. Header & Flavor Quotes

Name: Padania (LOM); as the Italian People's Federation under the People's Workers' Party.

Leader: Matteo Salvini (Lega Nord).

"Prima il Nord!"

Focus Tree & Path Overview

Target ideology: any of eight governments, from the Comunisti Padani to the Unione Nazionalista Padana.

Overview: the northern separatists. Padania wants independence, not the rest of Italy: it fights only Rome. After Victory over Roma Ladrona the player picks one of eight mutually exclusive governments, each with its own branch.

3. Lore & Geopolitical Goals

Background: the old dream of the Lega Nord comes back when Rome collapses: Lombardy and the Veneto break away and the Guardia Padana takes to the streets.

Goals: beat "Roma Ladrona", then decide whether Padania is a nation of its own (cores on Piedmont, the Aosta Valley, Liguria, Lombardy, the Veneto, Trentino, Friuli-Venezia Giulia and Emilia-Romagna) or the core of a new Italy (claims on every Italian state).

7. Unique Events & Mechanics

  • Padania is played by picking it in Italy Tears Itself Apart.
  • It stays out of the ideological wars: at the start it fights only the Republic.
  • Victory over Roma Ladrona needs peace with ITA, so the war with Rome has to end first.

The six-faction civil war (ITA, INT, SRI, RSI, LOM, TYR) is in the mod but, in the current version, it can only be started from the debug decisions: no focus triggers it yet.
